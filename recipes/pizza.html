Pizza

Description

The Italian classic. mozzarella Pizza

Nothing weird. just pizza

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • mozzarella
  • Basil

Guide

  1. Spin the pizza dough like a maniac until its round
  2. Add a glob of the deep red blood like tomato sauce ontop and spread it
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and any other toppings you like
  4. Bake in the oven until the cheese is melted and you cant wait anymore because you decided not to eat anything all day and now its midnight and you have work in the morning.
  5. Enjoy!!!!!
