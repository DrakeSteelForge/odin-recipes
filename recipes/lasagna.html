Lasagna

Description

Here we have the most luxurious Lasagna with.... a secret ingredient.

After you finish you won't want to eat anything else again

Ingredients

  • Pasta sheets
  • Tomato sauce
  • Minced beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Anything else you want
  • Souls of children

Guide

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Cook ground beef, onion, and garlic in a large skillet over medium heat until the meat is browned.
  3. Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, and oregano and stir until well combined and heated through.
  4. Stir mozzarella, cottage cheese, and Parmesan together in a large bowl.
  5. Spoon a layer of the meat mixture onto the bottom of a slow cooker.
  6. Add a double layer of uncooked lasagna noodles, breaking noodles to fit into cooker as needed.
  7. Top noodles with a portion of cheese mixture.
  8. Repeat the layering of sauce, noodles, and cheese until all the ingredients are used.
  9. Cover and cook on Low until lasagna noodles are tender, about 4 to 6 hours.
  10. Just before plating. Add ground The Childrens Souls on top
